Licensed Customs Broker N°1426

DHINA ELMOSTAFA — Daoufys Transit SARL. Your licensed broker for all import & export customs formalities in Morocco.

What is a licensed broker?

A broker is a physical or moral person who performs for others the formalities related to the detailed declaration of imported or exported goods.

The exercise of the brokerage profession is subject to obtaining a licence from the Ministry of Finance. For a company, the licence is required for the company and for any person authorized to declare on its behalf.

As a customs broker, we have the competence to perform customs formalities, and are considered liable for duties and taxes and criminally responsible for irregularities in a declaration. We are auxiliary to the Customs Administration, subject to the Discipline Chamber.

At export: our missions

  • Fret reservation (air or sea)
  • Goods pickup from clients for transport to embarkation points (port or airport)
  • Recording, customs clearance and handling formalities in customs offices
  • Follow-up, management and settlement of their temporary admission accounts

At import: our operations

  • Customs clearance, transit and handling in port, airport and extra-port warehouses under customs supervision or private
  • Goods pickup and transport to your warehouses
  • Personalized management of economic customs regimes
  • Delivery in the shortest delays following customs mainlevée

Mastered customs regimes

Putting into consumption, simple or temporary exports, transit, temporary admission, active and passive perfectionism, customs warehouses, home customs clearance, licences, visas, sensitive and regulated products (veterinary, phytosanitary, ONSSA, EACCE).
